# User Journey Map: The 5-7 Minute "Quote-First" Flow

**Persona:** Value-conscious Indian Homeowner.
**Context:** Has 10-15 opaque quotes. Frustrated. Wants the "real price."

| Step | User Action | User Mental State | Confusion / Friction Points | Clarity Added (The Solution) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Landing** | Arrives seeking a quote. | **Skeptical.** "Is this another lead gen trap? Will they just ask for my number?" | "Where are the pretty pictures? Is this a design site?" | **Value Prop:** "Get a line-item BOQ in 5 mins. No sales calls." <br> **Visuals:** Shows a sample BOQ table, not a living room render. |
| **2. Upload** | Uploads floor plan (PDF/Img) or selects "I don't have one" (fallback). | **Anxious.** "Is my plan good enough? Will it work?" | "My PDF is blurry." / "I only have a hand sketch." | **Feedback:** "Plan detected. Analysing rooms..." <br> **Guidance:** Simple "Snap a photo" instruction. Fallback to manual room add if upload fails. |
| **3. Room Confirm** | Reviews detected rooms (e.g., "Master Bedroom - 12x14"). | **Validating.** "Did the AI get it right?" | "It missed the balcony." / "That's a study, not a bedroom." | **Control:** Simple table list of rooms with dimensions. User can Rename, Resize, or Add/Delete. Confidence score shown. |
| **4. Default Scope** | Sees list of standard items per room (Wardrobe, TV Unit, Bed). | **Curious & Guarded.** "Are you adding too much? I don't need a false ceiling." | "What is 'standard' scope? Does this include handles?" | **Transparency:** "Based on your room size, we've added a 3-door wardrobe." <br> **Action:** Toggle items On/Off. No deep customization yet, just scope presence. |
| **5. Cost Drivers** | Selects Material (BWR/BWP), Finish (Laminate), Hardware. | **Calculating.** "How does this change the price? What is 'BWR'?" | "Is 'Standard' bad? Will 'Premium' blow my budget?" | **Education:** Comparison table inline. <br> "Standard = 10yr life, Premium = 25yr life." <br> **Real-time:** Price ticker updates as they toggle. |
| **6. The Reveal (BOQ)** | Views the detailed cost breakdown. | **Judgement.** "Is this total realistic? Why is it X lakhs?" | "Why is the kitchen so expensive?" | **Granularity:** Itemized list. "Kitchen: ₹2L (Carcass: ₹80k, Shutters: ₹60k, Hardware: ₹40k...)". <br> **Exclusions:** Explicit "Not Included: Civil work, Electricals." |
| **7. Download/Save** | Downloads PDF or saves to profile. | **Relieved.** "Finally, a baseline to compare others against." | "What do I do next? Do I have to buy from you?" | **Freedom:** "Take this to your contractor or hire us." <br> **Validation:** "Want an expert to double-check this for ₹499?" (Optional). |

## Key Journey Principles
*   **Confidence Building:** Every step acknowledges the user's input ("We detected 3 bedrooms") rather than guessing silently.
*   **Friction as a Feature:** We ask for verification (Room sizes, Scope) to ensure the user owns the output. "You said this, so the price is this."
*   **No "Surprise" Costs:** Tax, installation, and transport are mentioned early or clearly labeled as exclusions until the final calc.
